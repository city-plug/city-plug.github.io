1. What City Plug is

City Plug is an online marketplace that lists businesses by city. Each business is run by its owner, who is responsible for its products, prices, descriptions and delivery. City Plug does not sell the products and is not a party to the sale between a customer and a business.

2. Your account

You must give correct details and keep your password private. You are responsible for everything done through your account. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used it. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account.

3. Payments and commission

Payments are processed by Paystack. City Plug never sees or stores your card or bank login details. City Plug keeps a 12% commission on each sale, and the business receives the rest in its bank account. The price you see is the price you pay, with no extra fees for customers. Paystack may take its own processing charges from the commission.

4. Rules for business owners

You may only sell items you are legally allowed to sell, and you must describe them honestly, with true prices and photos. You must deliver what customers pay for, mark orders as shipped only when they are, and reply to customers promptly. You must not sell illegal, stolen, counterfeit, dangerous or adult items. Verification is optional, and a verified badge is not a guarantee of quality. City Plug may remove any business or product that breaks these terms.

5. Orders and delivery

An order is created when payment succeeds. The business ships the order and marks it shipped. Once you have your goods, confirm with "I have received". Only confirm after you have received the order, because confirming closes the order chat.

6. Cancellations, refunds and complaints

If there is a problem with an order, contact the business through the order chat first. If it is not resolved, file a complaint from the order, with your order reference. City Plug reviews complaints and decides each case on the facts. Where a refund is approved, it is sent back through the original payment method, and the time it takes depends on Paystack and your bank.

7. Messages and conduct

Order chats are only for the customer and the business of that order. Do not post links, threats, abuse, scams or anything unlawful, and do not try to take payments outside the platform. Do not misuse the site, attempt to access other people's data, or interfere with how it works.

We collect what you give us and what the service needs: your name, email address, role and password (stored securely, never in plain text); for orders, your phone number, delivery address and order details; for businesses, the business name, city, category, description, address, pictures and bank payout details; for verification, a photo of the shop and, if you choose, a photo of an ID; and your messages and complaints. We use this only to run the marketplace: to create your account, process and deliver orders, handle complaints and refunds, prevent fraud, and contact you about your account and orders.

9. Who can see your information

A business sees the name, phone number, delivery address and note of customers who order from it, only for those orders. Customers see public business details. Verification photos and ID photos are private and are seen only by City Plug administrators for the purpose of verifying. We do not sell your personal information. We use trusted providers to run the service: Paystack (payments), Supabase (secure data storage and sign-in) and Resend (emails).
