Terms of use

For everyone

City Plug is a marketplace that lists businesses by city. Each business is run by its owner, who is responsible for its products, prices and delivery.

Payments and commission

Payments are processed securely by Paystack. City Plug keeps a 12% commission on each sale. The business receives the remaining 88% directly into its bank account. The price you see is the price you pay, and there are no extra fees added for customers.

For business owners

You may only sell items you are allowed to sell, and you must describe them honestly. You are responsible for delivering what customers pay for. City Plug may remove a business or product that breaks these terms.

Refunds and complaints

If there is a problem with an order, contact the business first. If it is not resolved, contact City Plug and include your order reference.
